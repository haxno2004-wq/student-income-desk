# Student Income Desk — basic 60-day plan

## Purpose
Test five small student-focused offers while keeping daily maintenance to roughly 10–20 minutes. Revenue is uncertain; this is an experiment tracker, not a promise of passive income. Start manually and avoid paid software or advertising until there is evidence of demand.

## Starter offers

1. **Italy arrival planner** — tailor a checklist to a city and student profile, with links to official sources. Validate by interviewing five students.
2. **Student budget calculator** — offer a simple free calculator, then test whether a city-specific budget pack is worth paying for.
3. **First-week checklist** — make a compact printable/digital guide; share a sample and measure signups or purchases.
4. **University email templates** — create practical templates for common admin questions; ask students which situations they need.
5. **Student setup session** — manually offer a short paid 1:1 setup session to learn the most urgent problems.

## Weekly sequence

- **Weeks 1–2: Discover.** Speak to at least five target students. Capture the problem, current workaround, and willingness to pay. Do not build all five products.
- **Weeks 3–4: Make samples.** Create one-page samples for the two offers with the strongest interview evidence. Share directly and collect opt-in interest.
- **Weeks 5–6: Ask for payment.** Offer a small, clearly described paid pilot manually. Record actual sales, refunds, and time spent.
- **Weeks 7–8: Focus.** Continue the offer with the clearest evidence and workable delivery time. Pause offers with no response. Only then consider automating fulfillment.

## Daily 10–20 minute check-in

1. Check questions and customer messages (5 min).
2. Ask one person for feedback or share a useful sample (7 min).
3. Record evidence and actual revenue in the dashboard (3 min).

Once a week, spend up to 20 minutes reviewing which offer earned genuine interest relative to time spent.

## Dashboard limits
The first version is a static browser dashboard. It saves checkboxes and manual evidence/sales entries in local browser storage on this device. It has no AI agent, online store, payment, email, social publishing, or scheduled workflow connected. Use official and current sources for immigration, visa, tax, and university requirements; do not present the guide as legal or immigration advice.

## Success measures
Track conversations, qualified signups, paid pilots, refunds, and delivery time. Treat only completed, non-refunded orders as revenue. Set a small personal target after learning your available weekly hours and initial audience; do not assume income will arrive within two months.
